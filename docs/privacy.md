# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Zenvu ("the app") is provided by Firdavs Valijonzoda ("we", "us"). Contact: [valijonzodafirdavs@gmail.com](mailto:valijonzodafirdavs@gmail.com).

## 1. What Zenvu is
Zenvu is a directory of publicly available live TV, radio and webcam streams. We do not host streams. When you play a channel, the app connects to the third-party server that provides it.

## 2. Using the app without an account
You can browse and play channels without an account. In this case we do not collect your email or any other personal information. Settings such as theme, playback preferences and the last selected country are stored only on your device. Favorites require an account.

## 3. Data we collect when you create an account
- Email address (used to sign in and to send password reset emails).
- A unique account identifier (user ID).
- Display name, if you sign in with Google.
- Your favorites: for each favorite we store the channel type, channel identifier, channel name, country and the time it was added.
We do not store your password in the app or in Cloud Firestore. Authentication, including the password check, is handled by Firebase Authentication.

## 4. How we use the data
Only to provide the account features: signing you in, saving your favorites and syncing them between your devices. We do not sell your data.

## 5. Service providers
- Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google LLC) store account data and favorites. The app does not choose a database location in its code; the location is the one selected for the Firebase project. Data is encrypted in transit. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.
- Google Sign-In, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
- GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) provides the channel catalog that the app downloads. GitHub may receive your IP address when the catalog is downloaded.

## 6. Third-party streams
When you play a channel, the stream provider (or YouTube, for embedded players) receives technical information such as your IP address and device details, according to their own privacy policies. We do not control these providers.

## 7. Retention and deletion
We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can delete your account and all associated data in the app (Settings > Account > Delete account) or by following https://valijonzoda.github.io/Zenvu/delete-account/. Signing out removes the favorites copy stored on that device. The copy in the account remains until you delete the account. Backups may keep copies for a limited time before they are overwritten.

## 8. Your rights
You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by contacting [valijonzodafirdavs@gmail.com](mailto:valijonzodafirdavs@gmail.com). Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

## 9. Children
Zenvu is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
