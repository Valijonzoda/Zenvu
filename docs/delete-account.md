# Delete your account and data

## In the app
1. Open Zenvu and go to Settings.
2. In the Account section, choose Delete account.
3. Confirm. You may be asked to enter your password or sign in with Google again.

This permanently deletes your account, your email address and your saved favorites.

## If you cannot use the app
Send an email to [valijonzodafirdavs@gmail.com](mailto:valijonzodafirdavs@gmail.com) from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and data and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.

## Only remove favorites
You can remove individual favorites in the app at any time without deleting your account.

## What is kept
Nothing is kept after deletion except copies in backups, which are overwritten after a limited time. Settings stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
