# Copyright and DMCA

Zenvu does not host or store any video or audio content. It lists links to publicly available streams provided by third parties. Removing a listing from Zenvu does not remove the stream itself, so you may also need to contact the host of the stream.

## How to report
If you are a rights holder or authorized to act for one, send a notice to [valijonzodafirdavs@gmail.com](mailto:valijonzodafirdavs@gmail.com) with:
1. Your name, company and contact details.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work.
3. The channel name, country and, if possible, the stream URL as shown in Zenvu.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the rights holder.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

## What we do
We review valid notices and promptly remove the identified channels from the catalog dataset that Zenvu downloads. The app does not record who submitted a listing.

## Upstream dataset
Zenvu's listings come from the Famelack public dataset. Requests can also be sent to dmca@famelack.com.
